How much wallpaper do I need?

Estimate whole wallpaper rolls from full-height drops, including trim allowance, straight or half-drop pattern repeats, and optional deductions for very wide openings.

Quick answer: US double roll: a 32 ft wall run, 8 ft ceiling, 20.5 in × 33 ft roll, 24 in straight repeat, and 4 in trim need 7 double rolls (19 drops, 10 ft cut length). Metric: a 10 m wall run, 2.4 m ceiling, 53 cm × 10.05 m rolls, 64 cm straight repeat, and 10 cm trim need 7 rolls (19 drops, 3 drops per roll).

How the calculation works

Subtract only wide skipped openings from combined wall width, then divide by roll width and round up for required full-height drops. Add trimming allowance to wall height and round up to a whole pattern repeat for straight matches. For half-drop matches, each additional strip cut from the same roll adds half a pattern repeat to align adjacent panels. Divide roll length to find whole drops per roll, then round required drops divided by drops per roll up to whole rolls.

Worked examples (US and metric)

Example 1

Metric: a 10 m wall run, 2.4 m ceiling, 53 cm × 10.05 m rolls, 64 cm straight repeat, and 10 cm trim need 7 rolls (19 drops, 3 drops per roll).

Example 2

US double roll: a 32 ft wall run, 8 ft ceiling, 20.5 in × 33 ft roll, 24 in straight repeat, and 4 in trim need 7 double rolls (19 drops, 10 ft cut length).

Example 3

Plain design: the same 10 m × 2.4 m metric room with no repeat fits 4 drops per roll and needs 5 rolls.

What this estimate does not cover

Measure the tallest wall height. Leave skipped openings at zero for standard doors and windows because full-height strips are still needed beside them. Sloping ceilings, alcoves, alternating two-roll half-drop hanging, and manufacturer-specific matching rules require a dedicated cutting plan.
