How to work out how much wallpaper you need

Follow this measuring sequence before ordering wallpaper. Calculating full-height drops instead of raw wall area prevents short orders caused by pattern matching and end-of-roll off-cuts.

Step-by-step measuring walkthrough

  1. Measure the width of each wall you plan to paper and add the widths together. Keep standard doors and windows in the total because full-height drops are still cut beside them.
  2. Only measure and subtract wide openings such as sliding glass doors or large arched pass-throughs where whole vertical strips are genuinely skipped.
  3. Measure ceiling height from the top of the baseboard or skirting board to the ceiling line at the tallest spot in the room.
  4. Check the roll label for roll width, roll length, pattern match type (random, straight, or half-drop), and pattern repeat distance.
  5. Allow roughly 4 in (10 cm) of total trim per drop for squaring the top and bottom edges during hanging.

What to enter in the calculator

  • Combined wall width: Sum the widths of all walls to be papered.
  • Wall height: Measure from baseboard/skirting to ceiling at the tallest point.
  • Wide openings to skip: Leave 0 for normal doors/windows; subtract only wide spans like sliding glass doors.
  • Roll width: Common Euro roll is 53 cm; common US double roll is 20.5 in.
  • Roll length: Standard Euro roll is 10.05 m; US double roll is 33 ft.
  • Pattern match type: Choose the match symbol printed on the roll label.
  • Pattern repeat: Ignored when Plain / random match is selected.
  • Total trim per drop: Combined top and bottom trimming allowance (typically 4 in / 10 cm).

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Dividing wall area by roll area. Area division ignores unusable off-cuts left at the end of every roll and frequently under-orders by 15% to 25%.
  • Confusing a US single roll (sold priced per single roll, packed as a double roll) with the actual double-roll length you cut from.
  • Forgetting half-drop pattern waste. An offset match shifts every second strip by half a repeat, which can reduce drops per roll from three to two.
  • Ordering from multiple print runs. Always check that every roll shares the same dye-lot or batch number before hanging.

Practical ordering checklist

  • Measure in the same unit system printed on the roll label, or switch the calculator between US/imperial and metric.
  • Confirm whether the product is sold as a single roll (16.5 ft), US double roll (33 ft), or standard Euro roll (10.05 m).
  • Keep one extra unopened roll when the design has a large repeat or the walls are out of plumb.
